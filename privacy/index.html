Privacy and editorial transparency

This notice covers the exhibition pages at exhibition.vipach.at. The main VIPACH website and linked external sites may have separate notices.

Who runs this archive?

The archive is maintained by VIPACH (Vienna Photo Art & Creative Hub). VIPACH operates within the Vienna Hungarian School, as part of the Zentralverband Ungarischer Vereine und Organisationen in Österreich (ZVR 079797621; Schwedenplatz 2, 1010 Wien, Austria). For privacy, editorial or image-rights enquiries, contact office@vipach.at.

What happens when you open a page?

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

To display images stored in Wix’s media library, your browser connects to the Wix image delivery service. In making that connection, the browser sends technical information, including your IP address.

The public exhibition pages do not run website analytics or advertising tracking scripts in their own code. Following an external link connects you to the linked service. The email links open your email application.

Explicit language links determine which version you see. At the neutral homepage, the site reads your browser’s preferred languages to select a supported version; this selection code neither stores your language preference nor sends your language list to a server.

Photographs, sources and corrections

The exhibition pages document the events. Where known, we credit the artist and identify the sources of the images and text. We distinguish original artists’ texts from editorial copy and translations.

The images for the two 2025 shows are displayed in this online archive on the basis of the organiser’s confirmation. Their display does not grant permission to republish or otherwise reuse them.

If you spot an incorrect name, caption, translation or source, or want to contact us about information or an image of you on the site, email office@vipach.at. Include the page address and identify the relevant image or text. Please do not send identity documents in your first message.
